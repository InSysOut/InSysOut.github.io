Rules of engagement

  • Credit. Diagnostic fees are credited in full to any build that follows.
  • Re-scope. If the intro call shows the problem spans several systems, we say so before you pay.
  • Handover. Handover is the default. You own the code and documentation; the retainer is optional.

Excluded, in writing

  • Implementation during a diagnostic
  • Open-ended data cleaning or migration
  • More systems or stakeholders than the agreed cap
  • Access to systems or records without written approval
  • Work beyond the written acceptance test
  • Any testing outside the signed pentest scope
